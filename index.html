About Apex

Craftsmanship that earns trust.

Family-founded in 1987, Apex Build Co. has grown into one of the region's most respected general contractors — but the way we work hasn't changed. Every project gets a single point of contact. Every schedule gets a real plan. Every budget gets respected down to the last line item.

We staff our own crews, self-perform critical scopes, and partner with the same trusted trades we've known for decades. The result is fewer surprises, cleaner sites, and buildings that stand the test of time.

How We Work

A process built for no surprises.

Most construction horror stories start with "we didn't know." We don't allow that sentence.

  1. 01

    Discovery & Vision

    We meet on-site, listen to your goals, and translate them into a clear scope, budget, and timeline envelope.

  2. 02

    Pre-Construction & Design

    Estimating, value engineering, and constructability review — so the design is buildable, and the budget is real.

  3. 03

    Build & Quality Control

    Our crews self-perform critical scopes. Daily logs, weekly owner meetings, and zero tolerance for "we'll fix it later."

  4. 04

    Handover & Support

    Substantial completion, full as-built documentation, and a 12-month warranty walk-through. We don't disappear.
